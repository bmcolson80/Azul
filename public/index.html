HOW TO PLAY

The Goal

Be the player with the most points when the game ends. Score points by placing tiles on your personal wall in smart patterns.

The game ends after the round in which at least one player completes a full horizontal row of 5 tiles on their wall.

The 5 Tile Colours

Blue ✦ Cyan ❋ Red ✿ Yellow ❊ Black ✺

There are 20 tiles of each colour — 100 tiles in total.

Each Round Has 3 Phases

  • Factory Offer — take turns picking tiles from factory displays or the centre pile
  • Wall-Tiling — complete pattern lines move a tile to your wall and score points
  • Next Round — factories are refilled and play begins again
New player? Read Picking and Scoring first — those two tabs cover everything you need to play your first game.

Factory Displays

Circular factory displays sit in a ring. Each is filled with 4 random tiles at the start of every round.

  • 2 players — 5 factory displays
  • 3 players — 7 factory displays
  • 4 players — 9 factory displays

Your Player Board Has 3 Areas

  • Pattern Lines — 5 rows on the left, 1 to 5 spaces long. Fill these during the factory offer phase.
  • Wall — the 5×5 coloured grid on the right. Each space has a fixed colour. Points are scored when tiles land here.
  • Floor Line — 7 penalty spaces at the bottom for tiles you can't or don't want to place.

Starting Player Marker ①

The first player each round to pick from the centre takes the ① marker onto their floor line. They go first next round, but it costs 1 point.

On Your Turn — Pick Tiles

You must do exactly one of these:

  • Pick from a factory — take ALL tiles of one colour. The remaining tiles slide to the centre pile.
  • Pick from the centre — take ALL tiles of one colour. If you're first to do this, also take the ① marker.
In the app: Tap a factory circle to select it. If it has multiple colours, a colour picker appears. Tap the centre area to pick from there.

Then Place Your Tiles

Put your tiles into one pattern line on your board:

  • You can only place one colour per row
  • You cannot add a colour to a row if that colour is already on your wall in that row
  • Tiles that don't fit go to your floor line
  • You may send all tiles straight to the floor line if you prefer
In the app: After picking, tap a pattern row to place tiles there. Tap Floor to discard them all.

Round Ends When…

Every factory display AND the centre pile are empty. Wall-tiling then happens automatically for all players.

Wall-Tiling (happens automatically)

  • A complete pattern line moves its rightmost tile to the matching colour space on the wall — then scores.
  • The other tiles in that completed line go back to the supply.
  • Incomplete lines stay on your board — keep building them next round.

The Wall Pattern

Each row has a fixed colour order. Each colour appears exactly once per row and once per column — you can never place the same colour twice in a row or column.

Row 1: B Y R K C
Row 2: C B Y R K
Row 3: K C B Y R
Row 4: R K C B Y
Row 5: Y R K C B

Floor Penalties

At the end of wall-tiling you lose points for tiles in your floor line. Your score can never drop below 0.

−1−1−2−2−2−3−3

Scoring a Tile Placement

Every tile that moves to your wall scores immediately:

Tile with no neighbours at all
1
Count the horizontal chain of linked tiles (including new tile)
+H
Count the vertical chain of linked tiles (including new tile)
+V

If the new tile touches neighbours in both directions, you score both counts added together.

Example: Your new tile connects to 2 existing tiles in a row (3 total) and 1 existing tile in a column (2 total).
Score = 3 + 2 = 5 points.

Strategy Tip

Placing tiles near existing ones scores much more than isolated placements. Building clusters is the key to high scores.

Triggering the End

When at least one player completes a full row of 5 tiles on their wall, the current round finishes normally, then the game ends.

Bonus Points at Game End

Each complete horizontal row of 5 tiles on your wall
+2
Each complete vertical column of 5 tiles on your wall
+7
All 5 tiles of one colour placed on your wall
+10
Tip: Completing a vertical column or a full colour set is hard but hugely rewarding — aim for these from early in the game.

Winning

Most points wins. Tiebreak: most complete horizontal rows. If still tied, the victory is shared.

AI Difficulty Levels

  • 🟦 Rookie — picks mostly at random. Makes mistakes, wastes tiles to the floor.
  • 🟨 Veteran — prioritises completing rows, avoids penalties, simulates wall scores before deciding.
  • 🟥 Master — hunts column bonuses, colour-set bonuses, and adjacency scoring throughout. Tough opponent.
